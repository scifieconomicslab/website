Generative ethnography is the name we use for our process. It brings together three strands that usually sit in separate rooms.

**Complexity economic theory** supplies the models. They treat an economy as many people, firms and institutions reacting to one another, so a rule change can be tried and its knock-on effects watched.

**Speculative futures thinking** supplies the imagination. It lets a place describe rules it does not have yet and follow them far enough to see what living under them would be like. Science fiction is one way of doing this.

**Inclusive community-building** supplies the people. Residents, officials and local firms take part from the first step, so the options and the stories come from the place and are not brought in from outside.

The nine steps in [[overview]] are how the three strands meet. The model tests an option, the story lets the town feel it, and the community decides.
