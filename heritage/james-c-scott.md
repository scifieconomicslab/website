James C. Scott's *Seeing Like a State* shows how high-modernist schemes to make societies "legible" to bureaucratic control destroy the tacit knowledge and informal institutions that make societies resilient. The Lab takes this as a warning: any economic model that claims to see the whole system is likely blinding itself to what matters most.
