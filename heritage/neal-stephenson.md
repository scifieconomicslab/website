Neal Stephenson's *Cryptonomicon* and *The Baroque Cycle* trace the deep history of money, cryptography, and trust from WWII codebreakers to modern digital currency. His work shows how economic systems are built on layers of trust, violence, and code — and how each layer constrains what the next can become. The Lab uses Stephenson to trace the genealogy of a place's financial architecture.
