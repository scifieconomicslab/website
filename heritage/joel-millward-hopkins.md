Joel Millward-Hopkins extends the material footprint approach to show how stocks of infrastructure and capital lock in future energy demand. His work on the "stock-flow architecture of sufficiency" gives the Lab a dynamic framework: today's infrastructure investments determine tomorrow's energy requirements and thus the room for maneuver in any transition.
