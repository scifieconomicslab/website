Eric Beinhocker's *The Origin of Wealth* reframes the economy as an evolutionary system — a process of differentiation, selection, and amplification operating on designs for solving human problems. The Lab uses Beinhocker to ground its complexity economics approach: the economy is not a machine to be optimized but a search algorithm for discovering what works.
