Hyman Minsky's financial instability hypothesis shows that long periods of stability encourage risk-taking that eventually triggers crisis. His distinction between hedge, speculative, and Ponzi finance gives the Lab a diagnostic for where a place's economy sits on the risk spectrum. The Lab uses Minsky to ask: what financial structures make a place resilient to the inevitable turn?
