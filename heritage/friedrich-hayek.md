Friedrich Hayek's *The Use of Knowledge in Society* argues that the price system is a mechanism for coordinating dispersed, tacit knowledge that no central planner could ever possess. The Lab uses Hayek to ask: what does a place's economy know that no model can capture? The answer shapes the Lab's insistence on participatory modeling — the people who hold the knowledge must be in the room.
