W. Brian Arthur's work on increasing returns and path dependence shows that in systems with network effects and learning-by-doing, small historical accidents can lock in one technology or standard over another, forever. The Lab uses Arthur to ask: what historical accidents locked this place into its current economic trajectory, and what would it take to unlock a different path?
