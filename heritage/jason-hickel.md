Jason Hickel's *Less is More* argues that degrowth in rich countries is a prerequisite for global justice — not just environmental sustainability. His work on unequal ecological exchange shows that the Global North's "green growth" depends on extraction from the Global South. The Lab uses Hickel to ask: does this place's prosperity depend on extraction elsewhere?
