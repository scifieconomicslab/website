Vernor Vinge's *A Fire Upon the Deep* imagines a galaxy divided into "Zones of Thought" where the laws of physics — and thus the possibilities for computation, intelligence, and economy — change with distance from the galactic core. The Lab draws on Vinge to ask: what are the "zones of thought" that constrain what economic models are possible in this place?
