Elinor Ostrom's Nobel-winning work demonstrated that communities can successfully govern common-pool resources without privatization or central regulation. Her design principles for polycentric governance — clear boundaries, graduated sanctions, nested enterprises — directly shape the Lab's approach to participatory economic design.
