Lois McMaster Bujold's Vorkosigan Saga spans decades of a single character's life across a galaxy of wildly different economies — from the feudal militarism of Barrayar to the uterine replicators of Beta Colony to the quaddies of Jackson's Whole. Her work shows how economic arrangements shape what kinds of lives are possible, and how individual agency navigates structural constraint.
