Seth Dickinson's *The Masquerade* series (starting with *The Traitor Baru Cormorant*) follows an accountant who joins an imperial bureaucracy to destroy it from within, using economics as a weapon of liberation and control. The series treats empire as an algorithm for extracting value, and resistance as a matter of subverting its accounting. The Lab uses Dickinson to show how economic categories — debt, tax, trade — can be weapons of empire or tools of liberation.
