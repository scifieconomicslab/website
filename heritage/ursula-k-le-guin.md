Ursula K. Le Guin's *The Dispossessed* presents an anarchist society on a moon orbiting a capitalist planet, not as utopia but as a working, struggling, imperfect experiment. Her fiction doesn't predict the future — it makes alternative social forms thinkable. The Lab uses her work to show that fiction is not escape; it is a method for stress-testing economic imaginaries.
