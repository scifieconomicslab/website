Econophysics is the idea that economic realities have a material basis, which, at the lowest level, consists energy, matter and information. Originally proposed by scholars such as Nicholas Georgescu-Roegen in the 1970s, it influences the views of many modern post-growth economists, including Julia Steinberger, Joel Millward-Hopkins and Jefim Vogel.
