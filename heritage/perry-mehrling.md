Perry Mehrling's "Money View" restores the hierarchical nature of money to the center of economic analysis: gold, central bank reserves, commercial bank deposits, shadow money — each layer a promise to pay the one above. His work on the hierarchy of credit and the dealer-of-last-resort function gives the Lab a framework for understanding how a place's financial architecture constrains its real economy. The Lab uses Mehrling to ask: where does a place's money come from, and who stands behind it?
