Peter Watts' *Blindsight* and *Echopraxia* explore the unsettling possibility that consciousness is an evolutionary dead end — a metabolically expensive side effect with no adaptive value. His hard-SF exploration of cognition, agency, and the evolutionary origins of intelligence gives the Lab a provocative lens: what if the "rational agent" at the center of economic theory is a fiction that evolution has already discarded?
