Wassily Leontiev's input-output analysis mapped how every sector of an economy depends on every other — the first rigorous accounting of inter-industrial flows. The Lab's causal-loop models inherit this structural view: the economy is a network of dependencies, and you cannot change one part without tracing the ripples through the whole.
