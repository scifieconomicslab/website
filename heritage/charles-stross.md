Charles Stross' *Accelerando* follows three generations through the singularity, treating economics as a substrate that gets compiled, optimized, and eventually transcended. His *Merchant Princes* series explores trade, development, and institutional evolution across parallel timelines. The Lab uses Stross to stress-test economic concepts at the limits of technology and institutional change.
