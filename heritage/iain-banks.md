Iain M. Banks' Culture novels depict a post-scarcity society run by benevolent AI Minds, where scarcity is solved and the central human problem becomes meaning, not survival. The Culture serves as a thought experiment: what happens when material freedom is absolute? The Lab draws on this to ask what economic models should optimize for when scarcity is no longer the binding constraint.
