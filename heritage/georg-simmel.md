Georg Simmel's *The Metropolis and Mental Life* and his formal sociology of the dyad, triad, and web of group affiliations give the Lab a micro-foundation for understanding how social structure emerges from interaction. The Lab uses Simmel to model how economic behavior changes when people are embedded in different network structures — from tight-knit communities to anonymous markets.
