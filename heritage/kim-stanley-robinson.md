Kim Stanley Robinson's *The Ministry for the Future* opens with a heatwave that kills millions, then follows a UN agency tasked with defending future generations. Its central innovation — a "carbon coin" backed by central banks to incentivize carbon drawdown — is fiction as policy design. The Lab uses Robinson not for prediction but for making the financial architecture of climate action thinkable.
