Ha-Joon Chang's *Kicking Away the Ladder* shows that today's rich countries used protectionism, subsidies, and state direction to industrialize — then denied those same tools to developing countries. The Lab uses Chang to ask: what policy space does this place actually have, and who is telling it what it cannot do?
