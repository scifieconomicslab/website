Geertz was an influential American anthropologist and ethnographer, known both for his field work and for his reflections on ethnographic methods. Hirschman was a German-American economist and the founding father of development economics. The two worked together at the project of a reunified social science during their tenure at Princeton's Institute for Advanced Study.
