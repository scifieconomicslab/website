Will Wright's SimCity and The Sims turned complex systems simulation into a mass-market toy, letting millions of people experience emergence, feedback loops, and unintended consequences firsthand. The Lab draws on Wright's design philosophy: a good simulation is not a prediction engine but a toy for thinking — a space where people can try futures and learn from the surprises.
