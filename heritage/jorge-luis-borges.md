Jorge Luis Borges' *The Library of Babel* imagines a universe of all possible books — most gibberish, some meaningful. The Library is an economy of information: the cost of finding meaning in infinite noise. The Lab draws on Borges to ask: in a world of infinite possible economic configurations, how does a place find the ones that work?
