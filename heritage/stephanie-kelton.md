Stephanie Kelton's *The Deficit Myth* brings Modern Monetary Theory to a broad audience, showing that a currency-issuing government faces no financial constraint — only real resource constraints. The Lab uses MMT to ask: what could a place do if it stopped asking "how will we pay for it?" and started asking "what real resources do we have?"
