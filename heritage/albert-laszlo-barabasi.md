Albert-László Barabási's discovery of scale-free networks — where a few hubs connect to many, and most nodes connect to few — revealed a universal architecture underlying the web, protein interactions, citation networks, and economies. The Lab uses Barabási's tools to identify the hubs in a place's economic network: the firms, institutions, and people whose position makes them leverage points for systemic change.
