Tynan Sylvester's RimWorld generates stories by simulating individual psychology, social relationships, and environmental pressures in a colony on a hostile planet. The Lab draws on Sylvester's design insight: the most compelling simulations don't simulate systems — they simulate the people inside the systems, and let the system emerge from their choices.
