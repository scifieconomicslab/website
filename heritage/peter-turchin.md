Peter Turchin's cliodynamics treats history as a dynamical system, using mathematical models to detect long cycles of instability and cohesion in empires and states. His structural-demographic theory — elite overproduction, popular immiseration, state fiscal crisis — gives the Lab a macro-historical lens for a place's current stresses. The Lab uses Turchin to ask: where is this place in the long cycle, and what does history suggest comes next?
