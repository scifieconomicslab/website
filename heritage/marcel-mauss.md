Marcel Mauss' *The Gift* revealed that exchange is never purely economic — it is always social, moral, and political. The obligation to give, receive, and reciprocate creates the bonds that hold societies together. This insight underpins the Lab's view that economic models must embed social reciprocity, not just utility maximization.
