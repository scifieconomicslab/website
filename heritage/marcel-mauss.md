In _The Gift_ and other work he used ethnographic methods to paint the picture of economies that did not look remotely similar to what economists of his time believed any economies must be. He invented the signature move of economic anthropology: looking at historical and ethnographic records, and observe that they contradict economic orthodoxy. He was also a committed socialist, and active in the cooperative movement.
