L. Randall Wray is a principal architect of Modern Monetary Theory, developing the analytical framework for understanding money as a creature of the state — a tax credit that the state accepts in payment of obligations. His work on the job guarantee as a price anchor and buffer stock gives the Lab a concrete policy tool for a place that wants to eliminate involuntary unemployment.
