Its name evokes the supposed ability of truth to infiltrate the most difficult barriers. This idea resurfaced several times in the history of science, only to find a mature expression in the [Santa Fe Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Fe_Institute). From the SFI, the Lab derives its embrace of transdisciplinarity and eclecticism, as well as its attention to epistemology.
