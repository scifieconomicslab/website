Julia Steinberger's work quantifies the material and energy flows required for decent living standards, showing that the energy required for a good life is far below current consumption in rich countries. Her "energy-service nexus" framework — linking energy, materials, and human wellbeing — gives the Lab a biophysical floor for any economic model.
