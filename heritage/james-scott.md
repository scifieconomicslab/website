In his masterful _Seeing Like a State_, Scott observed that making models of reality implies simplifying it. This is normally innocent; but authoritarian states have the power to push their simplified views onto reality itself When that happens, great suffering and huge inefficiency ensue, as powerless people need to underwrite the distortions imposed by the regime. A prime example of this process, according to Scott, was the collectivization of agriculture in Russia undder Stalin. He further observed that many of these schemes are well-meaning, but still fail because they are blind to social reality.
