Muki Haklay's Extreme Citizen Science (ExCiteS) puts the tools of scientific inquiry — mapping, monitoring, modeling — directly in the hands of communities facing environmental injustice. The Lab uses ExCiteS as a model for participatory economic modeling: the people who live the economy should be the ones building the model.
