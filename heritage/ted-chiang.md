Ted Chiang's *Story of Your Life* (basis for *Arrival*) explores what happens when you know the future — not as prediction but as a completed whole. His *Exhalation* explores a world of mechanical beings who run on pressurized air, where thermodynamics is theology. The Lab draws on Chiang to ask: what changes in a place's economy when the future is not a risk to manage but a story already told?
