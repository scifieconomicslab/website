Joseph Schumpeter's theory of creative destruction — the entrepreneur as the agent who disrupts existing equilibria and creates new ones — gives the Lab a dynamic theory of economic change. The Lab uses Schumpeter to ask: what structures in a place's economy allow or block the emergence of new, better ways of doing things?
