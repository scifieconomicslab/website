Mariana Mazzucato's work on the entrepreneurial state and mission-oriented innovation shows that the most transformative technologies — from the internet to GPS to mRNA vaccines — emerged from public investment directed at bold missions, not market forces alone. The Lab uses Mazzucato to ask: what missions should a place set for itself, and how can public investment steer private innovation toward public purpose?
