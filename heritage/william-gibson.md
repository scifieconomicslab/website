William Gibson's *Neuromancer* launched cyberpunk with the line "the street finds its own uses for things" — a perfect summary of how economic agency emerges from the bottom up. His *The Peripheral* explores a future where the past can be accessed as a simulation, creating a market for historical outcomes. The Lab draws on Gibson to see the economy as a layer of code running on social reality, constantly hacked by its users.
