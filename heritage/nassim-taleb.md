Nassim Taleb's concept of antifragility — systems that gain from stressors, volatility, and disorder — gives the Lab a design principle for economic resilience. The Lab uses Taleb to ask: does this place's economy get stronger when stressed, or does it break? The answer determines whether the model needs more redundancy, diversity, and optionality.
