Alberto Cottica co-founded Edgeryders as an open ethnography platform where communities document their own lives and economies. The Lab's method of generative ethnography — where the place names the problem, the model shows the trade-offs, and fiction makes it felt — descends directly from this practice of communities as co-researchers, not subjects.
