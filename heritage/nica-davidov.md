Nica Davidov's work in environmental anthropology focuses on human-nature relations and indigenous knowledge systems as forms of environmental intelligence. Her work reminds the Lab that economic models of place must account for the more-than-human world — the rivers, forests, and soils that sustain the economy but do not appear in its accounts.
