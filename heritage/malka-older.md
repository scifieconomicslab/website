Malka Older's *Infomocracy* imagines a world of "centenal" micro-democracies (100,000 people each) governed by a global information bureaucracy. The system treats information as a public good and bureaucracy as a platform for democratic choice. The Lab draws on Older to ask: what would a place's economy look like if information were a public utility and governance a service?
