Stuart Kauffman's concept of the "adjacent possible" — the set of all possible next steps from a given state — reframes evolution as the exploration of an expanding space of possibilities. The Lab uses this to frame economic innovation: the question is not "what is optimal?" but "what is adjacent?" — what can this place do next, given what it already has?
