Thomas Schelling's segregation model showed that mild individual preferences for "not being in the minority" produce total segregation at the macro level — a seminal demonstration of emergence. The Lab uses Schelling to show that aggregate outcomes in a place's economy can be radically different from individual intentions, and that simulation is the only way to see it.
