François Quesnay's *Tableau Économique* (1758) was the first visual model of an economy as a circular flow — farmers, landowners, and artisans connected by flows of produce, rent, and money. The Lab's method of mapping causal loops in a place's economy descends directly from this insight: the economy is a circulatory system, not a factory.
