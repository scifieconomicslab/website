## What it is

A series of publications, including two peer-reviewed academic articles, proposing a mixed methods research that applies network science methods to ethnographic corpora. The goal is to highlight what, in the latter, constitute collective intelligence.

## What we did

We developed the method and some software to run it in a semi-automated way. We then used it in several research projects, on subjects ranging from community-provided health and social care to the directions that various hi-tech and activist communities are taking digital technology, to the analysis of European populism.

## How it turned out

Large, high-impact ethnographic studies. The application of network science allows to organize larger volumes of ethno data than previously possible, or at least practical.
