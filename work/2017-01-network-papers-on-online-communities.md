## What it is

Alberto Cottica leads a line of research on reading online conversations as networks. A 2020 paper, _Semantic social networks: a new approach to scaling digital ethnography_, proposes treating online conversations as communities and representing them as networks of key concepts and their associations as participants perceive them. Network science then finds the conceptual constructs where participants converge. The technique has been applied to several large ethnographic corpora, among them community-provided health and social care, European digital policy, the rise of populist politics in Europe, innovation in methods of political participation, and economic reform as professional politicians see it.

## What we did

Alberto and co-authors published a series of such papers between 2016 and 2023.

## How it turned out

The method is the one the Lab uses to read communities, and the wiki-as-data work applies it to books and concepts.
