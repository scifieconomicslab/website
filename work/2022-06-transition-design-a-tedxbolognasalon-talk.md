## What it is

A talk by Azzurra Spirito at TEDxBolognaSalon on 30 June 2022 at the Teatro Manzoni in Bologna, an independently organised TEDx event run by Andrea Pauri. The talk was titled Transition design: the transformative force of the future. The event page introduces her as a transformative innovation expert who helps organisations develop long-term thinking, build shared visions, explore alternative futures and design collaborative innovations, and who teaches at several universities.

## What we did

Azzurra presented futures and design methods to a general audience. The other speakers were a writer and molecular biologist, a strategic philanthropy consultant, the director of the IOM Mediterranean Coordination Office in Rome, an environmental justice expert, the national spokesperson of Fridays for Future Italy and a business partnerships lead at Treedom.
