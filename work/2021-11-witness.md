## What it is

Witness is a fictional, open-source floating city in which different economic systems coexist, based on an idea by Yudhanjaya Wijeratne. It is split into Distrikts such as Hygge, a welfare state, The Covenant, a religious economy, and The Assembly, an anarchist collective.

## What we did

The Lab and Edgeryders built the world with participants, designed a card game on top of it and wrote the Witnesspedia, a 2024 PDF guide to the universe. Players run a Distrikt through events such as pandemics and market crashes. The game ends after ten event cards or when an indicator hits zero, and players then discuss their policy choices. Later work grew from it, including A Town by the Sea and the Turku paper.

## How it turned out

The sources we read do not report player numbers or an evaluation. Later work in the same world includes A Town by the Sea and the Turku paper.
