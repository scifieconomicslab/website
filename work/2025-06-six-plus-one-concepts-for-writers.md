## What it is

A short slide set for fiction writers, posted to Edgeryders on 11 June 2025 after a workshop led by a colleague at Istanbul Innovation Days. The organiser had asked for five concepts, and the slides give six from economics and one general rule.

## What we did

The seven items, each with the fiction that illustrates it:

| Concept | What it says | Where fiction uses it |
|---|---|---|
| Leontief's input-output model | Maps how sectors supply one another, useful for planning and for controlling inflation | Robinson's Mars trilogy, Le Guin's The Dispossessed |
| Modern Monetary Theory | How government spending and money creation work | Doctorow's The Lost Cause, with its local currencies |
| Job guarantee | The state guarantees work to anyone who wants it | Doctorow's Green New Deal world, McAuley's Quiet War series |
| Shared real estate ownership | Housing cooperatives and community land trusts, common in Switzerland, Denmark, the USA and the UK | Robinson's New York 2140 and 2312 |
| Needs-based approach | Judge performance by how well human needs are met, often in non-monetary terms such as the Human Development Index | |
| Incentive compatibility | A system has to work with ordinary, flawed people, not only altruists | Doctorow's Walkaway, Robinson's The Ministry for the Future |
| Avoid unobtainium | Do not assume general AI or blockchain will solve problems they are not already solving | |

## How it turned out

The slides are public and reusable. The source records no audience outcome, and the workshop slot was very short.
