## What it is

A planned fully online event, announced on Edgeryders on 8 April 2021 for late November 2021 and still "tbc" at the time. It was a programme of online activities, described as a way to accelerate on-the-ground experimentation. It pairs creative visions of desirable, plausible alternatives with the current reality and the lessons of ethnographic research. One example asks which ideal world could rival the narrative of ethnonationalist authoritarians.

## What we did

Edgeryders and its consortium partners planned it in three tracks. Track 1, imagining desirable and plausible alternatives, had participants create districts for the Witness world through a writing competition in the original languages of the target countries. Track 2, understanding today, had researchers, think tank members and journalists present findings in short pre-recorded lectures with live Q&A. Track 3, from here to there, combined the two in co-creation sessions to map action plans and policies. Sessions were 12-minute presentations followed by 40 minutes of discussion, plus fishbowls, small groups and plenaries. Curators from the NGI and POPREBEL projects were to invite their researchers. Yudhanjaya and Navin led game mechanics, Alberto events and scenarios, and Nadia recruitment.

## How it turned out

The planned outputs were a source book, a topic digest, Distrikt and Game Master kits, extension kits and an event report, funded partly by crowdfunding for the Witness kit. The page records no outcomes. Witness followed later the same year.
