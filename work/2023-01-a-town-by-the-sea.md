## What it is

An online role-play game set in Witness, the floating megacity the Lab built with Edgeryders. Players are citizens of a small seaside town who make decisions about their community, learn to let go and discover the town's soul. The Lab's own site describes it as a worldbuilding game about triage and climate grief. It was designed by Matteo Uguzzoni with two collaborators, and it is meant as an easy way into both role play and the Witness world.

## What we did

The team developed it over about a month inside the Playful Futures project and ran a dozen playtests with a mixed group of players before announcing it on Edgeryders on 10 January 2023. It needed only an internet connection and a desktop browser, and no role-playing experience. Players first attended a 60-minute prologue session, with sessions on 17 and 19 January at 7pm CET, and four prologues were planned. Ten play sessions of five players each were scheduled between January and March.

## How it turned out

The team reported that the playtests went well. The post gives no detailed rules and no results from the public sessions, so this note does not say what players decided.
