## What it is

A card game in the world of Witness, developed in public on Edgeryders. A post of 14 August 2021 sets out an Internet Futures Edition: live-streamed tournaments that ask how internet technologies might work in alternative economies and societies, and what the Internet should do for humanity.

## What we did

Edgeryders planned the tournaments for autumn 2021, filed the work under the NGI Impact Summit and prepared a role at the NGI Impact Conference on 29 November. A closed playtest was reported on 10 December 2021 from a session with four platform members and two outsiders, used to guide further design.

## How it turned out

The game went from idea to tested prototype within a season, and Witness followed. The playtest findings are in the thread and are not summarised here.
