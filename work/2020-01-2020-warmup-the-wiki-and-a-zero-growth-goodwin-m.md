## What it is

A warmup post for the Lab's 2020 programme with two activities: a seminar on a zero-growth Goodwin model, with notes online, and an overhaul of the wiki of selected economics-themed science fiction.

## What we did

The Lab held the seminar and revamped the wiki while it prepared its wider plans, which the post says needed two or three more weeks.

## How it turned out

The revamped wiki is the base of the later wiki-as-data work. The Goodwin seminar is an early example of the Lab running an economic model. A related thread on simulating an economy with endogenous preferences looked for co-authors.
