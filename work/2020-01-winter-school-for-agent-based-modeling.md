## What it is

A two-week online programme on agent-based modelling of social-ecological systems, with about four hours of interactive sessions a day. It is advanced: participants must already write NetLogo and complete a GitHub tutorial beforehand. Week one is lectures on human and collective behaviour, resilience and land cover change, plus training in reproducibility, model documentation and analysis, and the start of group projects. Week two is project work and presentation of results, with participant speed talks. Participants adapt and analyse stylised NetLogo models.
