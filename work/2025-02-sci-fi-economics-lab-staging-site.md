## What it is

A staging page posted on Edgeryders on 21 February 2025 in which the Lab introduces itself. It argues that economists, politicians and business leaders have largely stopped imagining fundamentally different economic systems, while the Lab and a small group of visionary science fiction authors have not, and it cites Cory Doctorow's Walkaway and Bruce Sterling on nomads, cheap open-source technology and reputation.

## What we did

We used a Discourse topic as a stand-in website while there was no single page that told the Lab's work as one story.

## How it turned out

It has been the Lab's public page since February 2025. This site replaces it.
