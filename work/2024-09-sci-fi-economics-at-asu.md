## What it is

A one-hour workshop on Wednesday 18 September 2024 at the Piper Writers House on the Tempe campus, with a livestream, hosted by the Lincoln Center for Applied Ethics and the ASU Worldbuilding Initiative. It was free and open to the public.

## What we did

J M Applegate and Andrew Dana Hudson led it. They started from a core problem in economics: researchers cannot run large experiments on new economic systems, so the effects of new policies are hard to predict. They asked how imagination and speculative fiction can help design better economic futures, what futures people want, how to invite others into them and what storytelling can offer as a model for an economy. Attendees were invited to worldbuild alongside the presenters.
