Applegate's essays take sharp positions, including on advertising and on named executives. The site should publish such pieces under the author's name with a clear label as an opinion essay, and keep the landing page, Method and Work with us pages free of them. Policymakers read the front door, and the essays are for readers who choose to go deeper.
