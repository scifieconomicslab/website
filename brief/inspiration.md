- [April Zhu, Fieldnotes](https://april-zhu.com/fieldnotes): uses color figures and statecharts to show our capability for visual analogy.
- [AirsupHQ/machine-skill](https://github.com/AirsupHQ/machine-skill): a skill to draw machines and render them into explainable components.
- [Neil Mather's commonplace](https://commonplace.doubleloop.net/): the idea of garden vs stream.
- [HyperCard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HyperCard): the ease of navigation.
- Obsidian's graph approach to linked notes and ideas.
