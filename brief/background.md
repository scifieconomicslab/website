The Lab began as an Edgeryders initiative "to get economics out of its box". It was prototyped in 2019, launched at a public event in Brussels with Cory Doctorow, and funded in 2020 through EIT Climate-KIC, with Fondazione di Comunita di Messina as a partner (Science Fiction Economics Lab, Worldbuilding Academy).

### The four principals

We have (currently) four principals.

**Alberto Cottica** is an economist whose research looks at online communities as networks that can be read as data. His papers on semantic social networks, digital ethnography and the signature of community management appeared between 2016 and 2023, including two in Applied Network Science. He sits in the leadership group at Edgeryders with a data science role, and the UNDP lists him as an R&D Specialist in its Accelerator Labs.

**J M (Joffa) Applegate** has been an Assistant Research Professor at Arizona State University since 2019 and sits on the graduate faculty in Complex Adaptive Systems Science. She trained first as a physicist, with a BS in 1990 and an MS in 1992, then took a PhD in applied mathematics with a complex adaptive systems concentration in 2018 (CV). Her research covers complexity and evolutionary economics, labor, monetary and political economy, and the theory of the firm, and she has published agent-based models such as Emergent Firms and Empathy and Power on CoMSES. The Complexity Economics Lab studies economies as complex adaptive systems, with partners including the Global Climate Forum and the Santa Fe Institute.

**Azzurra Spirito** is a designer, consultant and trainer in transformative innovation, working with design thinking, system design and futures and foresight methods (Corep profile). She led the inquiry-based R&I Lab and coordinated the X-Labs co-creation programme at LUISS Guido Carli, and her consulting clients include public administrations, startups, cooperatives, investors and companies. She teaches a Feltrinelli Education course on the transforming economy and writes for AgenziaCult on community spaces and participatory processes.

**Yudhanjaya Wijeratne** is a Sri Lankan author, programmer and data scientist based in Kandy, who also writes fantasy as Victor Konara (about, press). He co-founded Watchdog on 23 April 2019, relaunched it in January 2022 as an open-source intelligence collective, and saw Reuters use its data to map the 2022 protests. Earlier he did computational social science and language research at LIRNEasia and founded the technology publication Readme.LK. Recent recognition includes a Dangerous Speech Fellowship, being on the Forbes 30 under 30, a joint Gratiaen Prize in 2023, an Open Society Fellowship in 2025 and a seat on Sri Lanka's National AI Advisory Committee.

### What each principal brings

| Principal | Strengths |
|---|---|
| Alberto Cottica | A participatory research community (Edgeryders) now at 130,000 posts from 3,500 authors, network methods for reading it, and the expertise to build such coalitions of groups; inroads into the UN structure |
| J M Applegate | Generative agent-based models, including the General Housing Model on CoMSES, and the economic theory that fundamentally shapes the work; inroads into several adjacent communities of thought and work, from game dev to math |
| Yudhanjaya Wijeratne | Science fiction, data science, game design, and playable simulations such as the game-engine Colombo and the generation-ship sim; inroads into Sri Lankan policymaking |
| Azzurra Spirito | Cooperation processes, online and offline, that connect public institutions with communities and private partners, expertise in structuring projects and prototypes in fields from urban regeneration to healthcare |
