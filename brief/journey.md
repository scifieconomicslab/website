A beautiful landing page that has text about what we do:

- complexity-economics models with real rigor
- science fiction that makes the choices visible
- software, community-driven speculative ideations, and visualizations as powerful communication tools
- the ability to work on a policymaker's actual problem

The landing page also doubles as a showcase of lightweight scrollable models as visual demos paired with the text (start with Witness, add GreatRetroFit).

From there it:

1. Leads off, garden-like, to our work (all pulled and archived and on-site in a specific template).
2. Guides users to the Work with us page.
