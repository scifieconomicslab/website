The site serves potential partners first, because Alberto's priority is a page they can latch on to. Contributors and writers come second.

| Audience | Leaves the site believing | Lands on |
|---|---|---|
| Mayors, ministries and city officials | A small team can bring a working model of my town's problem to the next meeting | A place-based case study, then Work with us |
| Institutions and funders (UNDP, foundations, EU programmes) | This group fills a knowledge gap that the new economics leaves open: practical, place-based and participatory | The landing page and the Method page |
| Economists and complexity researchers | The models are rigorous, open and calibrated, and the fiction does not replace them | The Models index with code and data links |
| Writers and worldbuilders | Economics gives me better building blocks than genre tropes do | Concepts and Better LEGO Blocks |
| Practitioners running local alternatives | Someone documents places like mine and puts them in the global conversation | The Places section |
| Contributors and wiki gnomes | I can tend this garden from Obsidian and my edits will ship | Contribute |

Reading time for the first two rows is short and we want to grab attention quickly.
