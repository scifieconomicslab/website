An economy is a choice, and the Lab helps a place see its options, test them and tell the story of living in them before it commits.

Every page should be traceable to these ideas. The structure below is lifted from Alberto's three statements.

| Layer | Message | Proof the site must show |
|---|---|---|
| What we want | We want to contribute to the transformation of our economies. Economics is making progress, many places already do things differently, and a global community is working on the change together | A Places section that treats Costa Rica, Indonesia, South Africa and Sicily as viable alternatives, not laggards |
| How we are useful | We supply the knowledge that community is missing. It is practical and experimental where most new economics is theoretical, place-based where it is place-blind, and participatory where it is technocratic | Case pages that name a town, a decision and what the model said |
| How we are special | We chose complexity economics, cheap computation and science fiction on purpose. Together they give rigor, easy deployment, participation and a sense of community | A running model, a story set in the same world, and a workshop format anyone can repeat. Witness, a card game about governing imaginary societies through crises, is the existing proof |
| Where we are going | Any small-town mayor can plot a course within the latitude allowed, and take part as a full citizen in the global conversation with the Pikettys, the Stiglitzes and the institutions willing to engage | A Work with us page with concrete formats and one contact |
