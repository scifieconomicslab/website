summary: "What it takes to replace the toy numbers in the town model with a real town's data."
---

The Linden Cove model is a teaching device with invented numbers. A real place needs its own.

What we would ask for: employment and wage data by sector, the rent and housing stock, the municipal budget by department, population movement, and a few years of local weather and trade data. With those, the constants in the model (spending propensities, labour share, service elasticities) are fitted so that the model reproduces the town's recent past before it is asked about the future.

How long it takes is an open question for the team: a few weeks if the data exists in usable form, and longer if it has to be collected.
