# Ticker templates

Keep headlines under 100 characters, dry and local. A line with an unknown slot is dropped.

## ev_drought
- Cistern at {pct}% as the dry spell enters its first month
- Farmers eye the sky, and the sky declines to comment

## ev_storm
- Storm takes tiles off the harbour road, repairs due
- Linden Cove counts the lamps lost overnight

## ev_bumper
- Bumper harvest fills the quay with crates
- Greenhouse Co-op sells out of tomatoes by noon

## ev_cargo
- Freight line drops Linden Cove from its route
- Ship leaves port half empty, exporters worried

## u_high
- {u}% out of work, the highest in {n} months
- The job centre queue now reaches the bakery

## u_low
- Every shop is hiring and few can be found
- Wages rise as workers become scarce
