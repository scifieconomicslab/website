Yudhanjaya Wijeratne is a Sri Lankan science fiction author, programmer and researcher based in Kandy, who also writes fantasy as Victor Konara (about, press). He originated the Witness world. His debut novel Numbercaste appeared in 2017, The Salvage Crew in 2020 (a Washington Post and Audible bestseller), and the series continued with Pilgrim Machines in 2024 and Choir of Hatred in 2025. His short fiction has appeared in Wired and the Big Book of Cyberpunk, and he has had commissions from the Lancet and the Financial Times.

He co-founded Watchdog Sri Lanka in 2019 and has served as its CEO. It was relaunched in January 2022 as an open-source intelligence collective, and Reuters used its data to map the 2022 protests. Before that he was founding editor of the technology publication Readme.LK (2014 to 2016), worked in technical writing and early data science at WSO2 (2016 to 2017) and did computational social science and machine learning research at LIRNEasia (2017 to 2020). He co-founded the compliance software company Dossiers in 2022 and exited in 2025.

Recognition includes a Nebula Award nomination in 2018 for Messenger, written with R. R. Virdi, a TEDxColombo talk in 2018, Forbes 30 Under 30 Asia in 2021, an IGF Excellence in Narrative nomination in 2021 as a contributor to Neurocracy, a Dangerous Speech Fellowship in 2022 to 2023, a joint Gratiaen Prize in 2023 for The Wretched and the Damned and an Open Society Fellowship in 2025. In 2025 he was also appointed to Sri Lanka's National AI Advisory Committee, where he works on making government more accessible to citizens and on Sinhala and Tamil datasets.

## What they bring

Science fiction, data science, game design, playable simulations, inroads into Sri Lankan policymaking.
