# Writing audit: prototype-v3

No /unslop skill exists in this account, so the standard applied was the writing rules saved in your preferences: no em-dashes, no AI tells (stock phrases, triplet lists and structures, negative parallelisms, "moreover" style connectors, rhythmically uniform or staccato sentences), and no claims the sources do not support.

## Method
1. A pattern scanner over every site-facing Markdown, template and script file (landing, method, work, offers, heritage, sources, models, copy, layouts, ticker text).
2. A manual read of everything the visitor sees on the landing page, all eight method steps, the process-name pages and every work note.

## Findings and fixes
- Em-dashes and en-dashes: none remain in site material.
- Filler and stock phrases ("it is important to note", "at its core", "moreover", "furthermore", "in today's"): none found. "Transformative" and "journey" survive only inside titles or proper names.
- Contrast-comma and negative-parallel constructions ("not X, but Y"): found and reworded in the heritage notes (Debt, Player-model principle), the Simulation Dream source, the Linden Cove model, and the landing footnote.
- Unsupported outcome claims: the "How it turned out" paragraphs in the 28 work notes carried phrases such as "settled the format", "became the template" and "clearest". All were rewritten to say only what the cited source supports.
- Repeated boilerplate: the internal "SF-ECON revenue hypothesis" paragraph was repeated across the offers and replaced with one plain sentence. The eight method steps shared a closing formula; each now ends with a plain "Step N of [[overview]]." line.
- Cute lines: the "seedlings into evergreens" line was removed.
- How we work: all eight step notes and the six scroll captions were rewritten, and the process is now named generative ethnography (complexity economic theory, speculative futures thinking, inclusive community-building) on the landing page, in the overview and on a new explainer page.

## Not covered or still open
- The triplet-pattern scanner was noisy (many legitimate three-item lists), so triplets were judged by reading, not by count.
- The ticker headlines were spot-checked, not read in full.
- PLAN.md and the README are working documents and were not audited as site copy.
- Three work notes have no source link yet (TEDxBologna talk, Shata Diallo dialogue, GlocalCamp 2017), and several notes carry a "To confirm" box. Those are factual gaps, not style ones.
